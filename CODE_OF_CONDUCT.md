# Community standards

## Commitment to a respectful community

Members, contributors, and maintainers are committed to making this community
free from harassment. This commitment applies to everyone regardless of age,
body size, disability (visible or non-visible), ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity or expression, experience, education, economic background,
nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity, or
orientation.

We aim to engage in ways that keep the community open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy.

## Expected conduct

Examples of constructive participation include:

- Treating other people with consideration and empathy.
- Engaging respectfully with different perspectives and experiences.
- Offering useful criticism and receiving it in good faith.
- Owning mistakes, apologizing where appropriate, and learning from their
  effects.
- Considering the wellbeing of the wider community, not only individual
  preferences.

Examples of conduct that is not acceptable include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwanted sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insults, demeaning remarks, or personal or political attacks.
- Harassment in public or private spaces.
- Sharing someone's private details, including a home or email address,
  without explicit permission.
- Other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional environment.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Community maintainers clarify these expectations and apply them. They will
respond fairly and appropriately to behavior they consider harmful,
threatening, offensive, or otherwise unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove, revise, or decline comments, commits, code, wiki
changes, issues, and other contributions that do not meet these standards.
When appropriate, they will explain moderation decisions.

## Where these standards apply

These standards cover all community spaces and situations where someone
officially represents the project in public. Examples include using an
official email account or social-media profile, or serving as an appointed
representative at an online or in-person event.

## Reporting and response

Report abusive, harassing, or other unacceptable behavior to the maintainers
through the repository's private contact channel or GitHub's reporting
workflow. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and impartially.

Maintainers must protect the privacy and security of anyone who submits a
report.

## Possible responses

Maintainers will use the following community-impact framework when deciding
how to respond to a violation:

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** Inappropriate language or other conduct that is
unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private written notice identifying the concern and
explaining why the conduct was inappropriate. The person may be asked to
apologize publicly.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation resulting from one incident or repeated
actions.

**Consequence:** A formal warning that continued behavior will have further
consequences. The person may be barred for a defined period from contacting
those involved, including the enforcement team, whether in project spaces or
outside channels such as social media. Breaching this restriction may result
in a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Community impact:** A major breach of the standards, including persistent
inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A time-limited suspension of interaction and public
communication with the community. During the suspension, the person may not
contact those involved, including the enforcement team, publicly or privately.
Failure to follow the restriction may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Community impact:** A repeated pattern of violations, persistent misconduct,
harassment of an individual, or hostility or denigration directed at groups
of people.

**Consequence:** A permanent prohibition on public participation in the
community.
